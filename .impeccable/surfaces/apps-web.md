# Browser identity direction

Mode: Operate. Scope: apps/web identity flow. Code-led build. Existing brand
authority is limited to the name. User explicitly requested continued work and
recording questions; the skill's interview/visual-choice pause is therefore
replaced by a provisional direction, with Q-13 awaiting later feedback.

## Grounded candidates

1. Municipal service signage: clear names and compact aligned wayfinding; blue
   header, pale blue ground, sans typography and a fixed location marker.
2. Technical standards manuals: systematic hierarchy for daily reference; white,
   deep green, workhorse sans and precise definition rows.
3. Team planning wall: shared operational focus; slate, white, sunflower and a
   single active-work marker, conventional controls without physical imitation.
4. Airport service-desk wayfinding: immediate identification and a clear next
   step; ink navy, cool blue, white and a compact location indicator.
5. Public-library identity: quiet repeated use; paper white, plum, readable sans
   and generous grouping of related information.
6. Railway timetable typography: quick repeated scanning; white, forest green,
   tabular alignment and precise row rhythm without a timetable costume.
7. Corporate training publications: approachable instruction; white, indigo,
   modest type scale and explicit question/answer relationships.

Candidate 4 is assigned by seed:f6e79c570b8eee00. No reroll or claimed user
selection. Predictable alternatives avoided: promotional login split with

## Challenger judgments

- Reference-setting edition: declined on audience identification and product
  clarity; keep its discipline of grouping errors with their fields.
- Dark developer console: competitive on clarity, weaker on audience breadth;
  keep deliberate separation of session-ending action from account data.
- Factory catalog sleeve: declined on both axes; keep complete palette
  commitment without coded labels or invented identifiers.
- Cassette inlay: declined on both axes; keep aligned compact information.
- Zoo guide map: declined on both axes; keep decisive large-area color roles
  without organic map decoration or motion that distracts from login.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A clear entrance to an internal workplace. Identity is the task;
promotional panels and fake operational previews are excluded.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-navy masthead, cool blue page, white working surface, cobalt
primary action, readable system sans and compact conventional form controls.
Field groups and errors align. Session exit has its own separated area.

STORY: Recognize Altvia, sign in with a provisioned account, inspect the real
account and leave securely. Errors explain the next action without revealing
account existence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width masthead with product left and theme control right.
Centered, left-aligned 440px form on a spacious cool-blue ground. Heading,
email, password/reveal and full-width Entrar action lead. A small location
marker beside the masthead name changes from Acesso to Minha conta after real
authentication. Mobile keeps the same order and comfortable touch targets.

FORM: Candidate 4, airport service-desk wayfinding; seed:f6e79c570b8eee00.
Standard web form topology, restrained palette, no images, no decorative
motion. The location marker is the signature move and also communicates state.
