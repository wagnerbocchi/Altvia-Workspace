# Browser identity and shared workspace direction

Mode: Operate. The user-selected Twenty CRM direction of 2026-11-12 replaces
this brief's former provisional airport/service-desk world. The shared redesign
contract lives in apps/web/.impeccable/surfaces/apps-web.md; actual reusable tokens
and patterns live in root DESIGN.md and .impeccable/design.json. No approved
corporate logo or typeface was supplied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A clear entrance to a daily operational workspace. Identity is a real
task; promotional panels and fake operational previews are excluded.

OWN-WORLD: Offwhite sidebar, continuous white content, gray hairlines, system
sans and restrained blue actions. Dark mode uses neutral charcoal equivalents.
The signed-out shell uses the same neutral materials without authenticated links.

STORY: Recognize Altvia, sign in with a provisioned account, inspect the real
account and leave securely. Errors explain the next action without revealing
account existence. After authentication, navigate to real destinations within
the private session boundary. Navigation never grants resource access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Signed-out states retain a neutral masthead, current place and
theme control, with a centered left-aligned surface bounded at 440px. Heading,
email, password/reveal and full-width Entrar action lead. Authenticated content
uses the shared sidebar and context bar; account facts use a deliberate 640px
maximum and flat section boundaries. Mobile retains order and comfortable targets.
The skip link reaches route content; mobile navigation preserves keyboard entry,
Escape recovery and focus when the current destination is selected.

FORM: User-selected Twenty product UI, code-led. Translate precision and density
without copying its brand, source, assets or CRM features. Preserve provisioned
login, readable errors, real account values and separated session-ending actions.
No raster assets or decorative motion; existing loading respects reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
