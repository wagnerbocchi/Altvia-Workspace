then manages its independently persisted assignment and lifecycle.

flat section boundaries, current typography and visible keyboard focus.

assignment, reasoned state changes and immutable history.

Detail prioritizes subject/number/state, identity references and current actions.
Mobile follows the same stacked reading order with wrapping text and controls.
FORM Code-led operational extension under seed:f6e79c570b8eee00.
Retain original protected POST body/key/version
through uncertain responses and failed reads. Confirm authoritative state before
the next mutation. Keep requester identity visibly unverified.
